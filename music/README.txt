Put your own permitted audio files in this folder (mp3, m4a, ogg, wav).
Then list them in the playlist at the top of script.js, e.g.
  { title: "Dreamy Nights", artist: "Your Artist", src: "music/song1.mp3" }
Filenames are case-sensitive once the site is online (Song1.mp3 is not song1.mp3).
Or skip all this and use "+ ADD SONGS" on the page to test with files from your device.
